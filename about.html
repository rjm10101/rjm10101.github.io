About

Aloha kākou!

I’m Ray, a marketing leader and creative director based in Honolulu. Most recently I led marketing for Cirque du Soleil ʻAuana, the company’s first resident show in Hawaiʻi.

I’ve built my career on both sides of the work: planning media for Samsung and Mastercard at Starcom and Carat, then leading creative for entertainment, hospitality and consumer brands. I care about work that looks right and pays off, and I don’t see those as separate jobs.

I’m looking for my next role as a marketing director or creative director, in Hawaiʻi or remote. If your team needs both skill sets in one person, I’d love to talk. Email {{ site.email }} or give me a call.
